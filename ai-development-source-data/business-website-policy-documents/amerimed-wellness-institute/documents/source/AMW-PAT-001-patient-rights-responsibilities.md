## 1. Your rights as a patient
At Amerimed, you have the right to:

### Respect and dignity
1. Considerate, respectful care that honours your personal values, beliefs, culture and spiritual needs.
2. Care free from discrimination based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, age, disability, medical condition, genetic information, marital status, citizenship, primary language, immigration status, or source of payment.
3. Privacy during examination and treatment, and confidentiality of your health information.
4. Be addressed by the name and pronouns you choose.

### Information and decision-making
5. Know the names and roles of the people caring for you.
6. Receive information about your diagnosis, treatment, prognosis and unexpected outcomes in terms you understand.
7. **Free interpreter services**, including American Sign Language and 240+ spoken languages, 24/7, by phone, video or in person.
8. Take part in decisions about your care, including the development of your care plan and discharge plan.
9. Give or refuse **informed consent** for procedures, research and treatment, and refuse treatment to the extent the law permits.
10. Create an **advance directive** (e.g. a California Advance Health Care Directive, an Oregon Advance Directive, a Washington Health Care Directive or a POLST) and have it honoured.
11. Have a family member or representative and your own physician notified promptly of your admission.
12. Get a second opinion.

### Safety
13. Receive care in a safe setting, free from abuse, neglect, harassment and exploitation.
14. Be free from restraints and seclusion unless clinically necessary, and only according to law (42 CFR 482.13).
15. Appropriate assessment and management of pain.

### Visitors and support
16. Have visitors of your choosing, including a domestic partner, regardless of legal relationship (subject to clinical and safety limits).
17. Have a **support person** with you, as allowed by law and infection-control conditions.

### Records and billing
18. Access, inspect and receive a copy of your medical records, and request amendments (see AMW-PAT-002).
19. Receive a **good-faith estimate** of expected charges if you are uninsured or self-pay (No Surprises Act), and an itemised bill on request.
20. Apply for **financial assistance** (AMW-PAT-003), regardless of immigration status.

### Emergency care
21. Receive a medical screening examination and stabilising treatment for an emergency medical condition, regardless of your ability to pay (**EMTALA**).

## 2. Your responsibilities
- Give accurate and complete information about your health, medications, allergies and insurance.
- Ask questions when you don't understand something.
- Follow the agreed care plan, or tell your care team if you can't.
- Respect other patients, visitors and staff. **Amerimed has zero tolerance for violence, threats or harassment against anyone.**
- Keep appointments, or cancel at least 24 hours in advance.
- Meet the financial obligations agreed for your care, or talk to us about assistance.
- Follow facility rules on smoking (all campuses are tobacco-, cannabis- and vape-free), weapons and visitor guidelines.

## 3. Raising a concern or grievance
We want to hear from you. You can:

1. **Speak to your nurse or the charge nurse / clinic manager** - most concerns can be resolved quickly.
2. **Contact Patient Experience:** 1-888-555-0125 | patientexperience@amerimedwellness.example. We will acknowledge your grievance within **7 days** and send a written response within **30 days**.
3. **Contact the state or accreditation body directly**, whether or not you have used our process:

| State | Agency |
|---|---|
| California | California Department of Public Health - Licensing & Certification (district office for the facility) |
| Oregon | Oregon Health Authority - Health Care Regulation & Quality Improvement |
| Washington | Washington State Department of Health - Complaint Intake |
| Nevada | Nevada Division of Public & Behavioral Health - Bureau of Health Care Quality & Compliance |

Current phone numbers and online complaint forms for each agency are posted at every registration desk and on our website.

Medicare beneficiaries may also contact the Medicare Quality Improvement Organization (BFCC-QIO) about quality of care or discharge concerns.

Civil-rights complaints may be filed with the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services, Office for Civil Rights.
