## 1. Registering as a new patient
Register online through **MyAmerimed** (web or app), by phone at 1-888-555-0123, or in person at any clinic.

### What to bring or upload
- Photo ID (driver's licence, state ID, passport, consular ID, or school ID for minors). *ID is not required to receive emergency care.*
- Insurance card(s) - front and back
- List of current medications, allergies and immunisations
- Name of your previous primary care provider (we will request records with your authorisation)
- Advance directive or POLST, if you have one
- Legal documents for minors in guardianship or foster care

### Minors
Patients under 18 need consent from a parent or legal guardian, **except** for services that minors may consent to under state law. In California these include sensitive services such as reproductive health, mental health (age 12+), substance use treatment and STI care. Oregon and Washington have their own minor-consent rules (e.g. Oregon age 15+ for most medical care).

## 2. Insurance verification
- Eligibility and benefits are verified electronically 3 days before scheduled visits.
- If coverage cannot be verified, you will be offered self-pay rates, a Good Faith Estimate, and screening for financial assistance or coverage programmes.
- **Referrals:** HMO plans may require a referral from your primary care physician. Our referral coordinators manage this for in-network specialists.

## 3. Scheduling and access standards

| Visit type | Amerimed target | Regulatory standard (CA DMHC, for reference) |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent care (no prior authorisation) | Same day / walk-in | Within 48 hours |
| Non-urgent primary care | Within 7 business days | Within 10 business days |
| Non-urgent specialist | Within 10 business days | Within 15 business days |
| Non-urgent mental health (non-physician) | Within 7 business days | Within 10 business days |
| Mental health follow-up | Within 10 business days | Within 10 business days |

## 4. Cancellations, late arrivals and no-shows
- Please cancel at least **24 hours** ahead through MyAmerimed or by phone.
- Patients who arrive more than **15 minutes late** may be asked to reschedule, or offered a telehealth visit.
- After **3 no-shows in 12 months**, scheduling may be limited to same-day appointments. We never dismiss patients for no-shows without outreach and a review of barriers (transport, language, health-related social needs).
- Amerimed does **not** charge no-show fees to Medi-Cal, Oregon Health Plan or Apple Health members.
- **Free rides:** Medi-Cal and OHP members can request non-emergency medical transport. Amerimed Ride Assist covers other patients facing transport barriers.

## 5. Amerimed Anywhere - telehealth

### 5.1 Services available
Video and phone visits for primary care, urgent care (24/7 on-demand), behavioral health, dermatology (photo upload), chronic disease management, post-surgical follow-up, lactation support and remote patient monitoring.

### 5.2 Eligibility and licensure
- The patient must be **physically located in a state where the clinician is licensed** at the time of the visit (CA, OR, WA or NV). Clinicians confirm your location at the start of each visit.
- Prescribing controlled substances by telehealth follows the DEA telemedicine rules in effect, and state law.
- A telehealth visit may be converted to an in-person visit if clinically needed, at no extra charge for the telehealth portion.

### 5.3 Consent
Before your first telehealth visit you give verbal or written consent, as required by **California Business & Professions Code 2290.5**. Consent is recorded in your chart. You may choose in-person care at any time.

### 5.4 Privacy and technology
- Video visits use a HIPAA-compliant platform with end-to-end encryption, through MyAmerimed. No downloads are needed.
- Please join from a private location. Interpreters and family members can be added to the call.
- If the connection drops, your clinician will call you on your phone number on file.

### 5.5 Cost
Telehealth is billed to insurance like an in-person visit. **Payment parity** applies under California AB 457 and Oregon/Washington telehealth laws. On-demand urgent video visits for self-pay patients cost **USD 59**.

## 6. Patient portal - MyAmerimed
- View test results (released automatically under the federal 21st Century Cures Act information-blocking rules, with optional clinician-review delays for sensitive results in line with state law), message your care team, request refills, pay bills and download records.
- **Proxy access:** parents of children under 12 have full access. For ages 12-17, access is limited to protect confidential services. Adults can grant proxy access to caregivers.
- Non-urgent messages are answered within **2 business days**. **Never use the portal for emergencies - call 911.**
