**THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.**

## 1. Our duties
Amerimed is required by law to:
- maintain the privacy and security of your protected health information (PHI)
- give you this notice of our legal duties and privacy practices
- follow the terms of the notice currently in effect
- notify you promptly if a breach occurs that may have compromised the privacy or security of your information

## 2. Your rights

| Right | What it means | How to exercise |
|---|---|---|
| **Get a copy of your records** | Electronic or paper copy of your medical and billing records, usually within 15 days (California: 15 days; HIPAA maximum 30 days). We may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee. | MyAmerimed portal (free, immediate for most results), or the Health Information Management (HIM) department |
| **Ask us to correct your records** | Request an amendment if you believe information is incorrect or incomplete. We will respond within 60 days. | Written request to HIM |
| **Request confidential communications** | Ask us to contact you in a specific way (e.g. a different phone or address). We will agree to all reasonable requests. | Registration or the portal |
| **Ask us to limit what we use or share** | We are not required to agree, except when you pay out of pocket in full and ask us not to share with your health plan. | Written request to the Privacy Office |
| **Get a list of disclosures** | Accounting of disclosures for 6 years before your request (excluding treatment, payment and operations). One free list per year. | Privacy Office |
| **Get a copy of this notice** | Paper copy at any time, even if you agreed to electronic delivery | Any registration desk |
| **Choose someone to act for you** | A medical power of attorney or legal guardian can exercise your rights | Provide documentation |
| **File a complaint** | If you feel your rights are violated | See Section 7 |

## 3. Your choices
You can tell us your choices about the following:
- sharing information with family, close friends or others involved in your care
- sharing information in a disaster relief situation
- including your information in the hospital directory
- fundraising communications - you can opt out at any time

**We never share your information unless you give us written permission for:**
- marketing purposes
- sale of your information
- most sharing of psychotherapy notes

## 4. Our uses and disclosures
| Purpose | Example |
|---|---|
| **Treatment** | A doctor treating you for an injury asks another doctor about your overall health condition. We share records through health information exchanges (e.g. Carequality, CommonWell, state HIEs). |
| **Payment** | We give information to your health insurance plan so it will pay for your services. |
| **Health care operations** | We use information to improve care quality, train students and residents, and conduct audits. |
| **Public health and safety** | Disease reporting, product recalls, adverse drug reactions, preventing serious threats to health or safety. |
| **Research** | Only with your authorisation, or with Institutional Review Board (IRB) waiver approval. |
| **Comply with the law** | HHS compliance reviews; court orders and subpoenas; law enforcement (as limited by law). |
| **Organ and tissue donation** | With organ procurement organisations. |
| **Coroners and funeral directors** | When an individual dies. |
| **Workers' compensation, health oversight, special government functions** | As permitted by law. |

## 5. Specially protected information
Some information has extra legal protection, and generally requires your specific written authorisation to share:
- **Substance use disorder treatment records** (42 CFR Part 2, as amended in 2024): records from Part 2 programs at Amerimed Behavioral Health will not be used in proceedings against you without your consent or a court order.
- **Mental health records** (CA Lanterman-Petris-Short Act; WA RCW 70.02.230)
- **HIV test results** (CA Health & Safety Code 120980)
- **Genetic information** (GINA; CA Civil Code 56.17)
- **Reproductive and gender-affirming health care information:** under California Civil Code 56.108-56.110 and Washington's My Health My Data Act, Amerimed will not disclose information about legally protected reproductive or gender-affirming care in response to out-of-state subpoenas or law enforcement requests related to such care, except as required by law.
- **Minors' sensitive services** (consent and confidentiality under state law)

## 6. California, Oregon and Washington rights
Under the California Confidentiality of Medical Information Act (CMIA), Oregon ORS 192.553-192.581 and the Washington Uniform Health Care Information Act, you may have additional rights. Where state law is more protective than HIPAA, we follow state law.

## 7. Complaints
You can complain if you feel we have violated your rights:
- **Amerimed Privacy Office:** 2800 Pacific Crest Boulevard, San Jose, CA 95134 | 1-888-555-0131 | privacy@amerimedwellness.example
- **U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights:** 200 Independence Avenue SW, Washington, DC 20201 | 1-877-696-6775 | www.hhs.gov/ocr/privacy/hipaa/complaints/
- **California Department of Public Health** (for CMIA concerns)

We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

## 8. Changes to this notice
We can change the terms of this notice, and the changes will apply to all information we have about you. The new notice will be available on request, at our facilities and on our website.
