About Amerimed

A nonprofit integrated health system serving California, Oregon, Washington and Nevada.

Our story

Amerimed began in 1964 as the Pacific Crest Wellness Clinic in San Jose, founded by three physicians and a public health nurse to bring preventive care to farm-worker and factory families in the Santa Clara Valley.

Today Amerimed Wellness Institute has 26,500 caregivers, six hospitals from Long Beach to Seattle, and one of the West Coast's largest telehealth programmes. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, we reinvest every dollar in care, community health and education for the next generation of clinicians.

Mission: To deliver whole-person care, close to home, for every community we serve - regardless of ability to pay.

Our values

Compassion

We treat every patient as we would want our families treated.
