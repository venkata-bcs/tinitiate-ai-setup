Frequently asked questions

Answers to common patient questions about appointments, billing and privacy.

Do I need insurance to be seen?

No. Emergency care is provided regardless of ability to pay. Uninsured patients get a 40% self-pay discount, a Good Faith Estimate and screening for financial assistance.

Who qualifies for free care?

At our California hospitals, households up to 300% of the federal poverty level qualify for free care, with discounts above that. Oregon and Washington have their own scales. See AMW-PAT-003.

How do I get a copy of my medical records?

Most records are available instantly in MyAmerimed. Otherwise contact Health Information Management; we respond within 15 days. See AMW-PAT-002.

I got a bill from an out-of-network doctor at an Amerimed hospital. Do I have to pay it?

For emergency care and most care at in-network hospitals, you owe only your in-network cost-sharing. Call 1-888-555-0127 or the No Surprises Help Desk at 1-800-985-3059.

Can I use telehealth when I'm travelling?

Only when you are physically in a state where your clinician is licensed (CA, OR, WA or NV).

Can a teenager see a doctor without a parent?

For some sensitive services, yes, depending on state law. In California, minors 12+ can consent to certain mental health and reproductive health services.

Will Amerimed report my medical debt to credit bureaus?

No. Amerimed does not report medical debt to credit bureaus at any facility.

How do I raise a concern about my care?

Speak with your nurse or clinic manager, or call Patient Experience at 1-888-555-0125. You can also contact the state health department directly.
