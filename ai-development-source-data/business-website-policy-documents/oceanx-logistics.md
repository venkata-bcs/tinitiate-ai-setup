## Explainer

OceanX Logistics Pte. Ltd. is a Singapore-headquartered container shipping line, founded in 1998 with two feeder ships. It now runs **59 vessels (412,000 TEU)** on six trade lanes and serves **214 ports**. It has regional entities in **China, the USA, the EU (Netherlands) and India**, so its policies must handle multi-currency payments, five tax regimes, advance-manifest customs rules (AMS/ISF, ICS2, China Advance Manifest, ICEGATE), maritime safety (ISM, ISPS, MARPOL, IMDG) and seafarer welfare (MLC 2006, STCW).
