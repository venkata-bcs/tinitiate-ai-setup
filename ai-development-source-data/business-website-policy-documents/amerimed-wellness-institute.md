## Explainer

Amerimed Wellness Institute began in 1964 as a single preventive-care clinic in San Jose. It is now a **501(c)(3) integrated health system** with:
- **6 hospitals (2,140 beds)**
- **48 clinics** and **22 urgent care centres**
- a 24/7 telehealth service, **Amerimed Anywhere**
- operations across **California, Oregon, Washington and Nevada**

Its policies cover the main healthcare compliance areas:
- **HIPAA** privacy and security, plus state laws (CMIA, 42 CFR Part 2, WA My Health My Data)
- **EMTALA**
- **IRS 501(r)** and state charity-care laws
- the **No Surprises Act** and **price transparency**
- **Anti-Kickback Statute, Stark Law and False Claims Act**
- CMS **infection control** Conditions of Participation
- California nurse-staffing ratios and workplace-violence rules
