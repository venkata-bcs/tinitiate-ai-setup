About OceanX

A Singapore-headquartered carrier connecting Asia with the Americas, Europe and the Indian Subcontinent since 1998.

Our story

OceanX was founded in 1998 by a group of Singaporean ship managers with two 1,100 TEU feeder vessels serving Port Klang, Jakarta and Singapore. Strong intra-Asia growth led to the first deep-sea service, the Transpacific "PNX" loop, in 2006.

Today OceanX Logistics Group operates 38 owned and 21 chartered container ships, employs 4,850 shore staff and 6,200 seafarers, and runs its own terminals in Singapore and Navi Mumbai. Our regional hubs in Shanghai, Long Beach and Rotterdam bring us close to customers in every major trade lane.

Mission: Move our customers' cargo safely, reliably and sustainably, while caring for the people who make it happen at sea and ashore.
