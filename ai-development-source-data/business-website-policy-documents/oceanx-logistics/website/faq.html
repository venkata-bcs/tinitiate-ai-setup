Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions our customer service teams hear most often.

How do I open an account with OceanX?

Register on OX Navigator and upload your company registration, tax ID and authorised signatory ID. Most accounts are approved within 3 business days. See OX-CUS-001.

Which payment methods do you accept in China?

Domestic bank transfer (CNAPS), corporate online banking and, for amounts up to CNY 50,000, Alipay or WeChat Pay corporate accounts. Ocean freight in USD needs SAFE-compliant documents. See OX-CUS-002.

Can I pay by UPI in India?

Yes. UPI for business is accepted up to INR 2 lakh per transaction. NEFT and RTGS are available for larger amounts.

How long is free time on import containers in the USA?

4 days of demurrage and 4 days of detention on the West Coast; 5 and 4 days on the East and Gulf Coasts. Reefers receive half. See OX-CUS-005.

How do I dispute a demurrage invoice?

Use OX Navigator > Billing > Dispute within 30 days of the invoice date. Pay the undisputed amount; the disputed part is put on hold. See OX-CUS-003.

Do you ship lithium batteries?

Yes, as Class 9 dangerous goods with a lithium battery declaration. Damaged or recalled batteries are never accepted. See OX-CUS-006.

What is the SI cut-off?

48 hours before vessel departure for cargo bound for the US, Canada, the EU and China, because of advance manifest rules.

How much is OceanX liable for if my cargo is damaged?

Under the Hague-Visby Rules, 666.67 SDR per package or 2 SDR per kg, whichever is higher. US COGSA limits liability to USD 500 per package. We recommend cargo insurance. See OX-CUS-007.

Someone emailed me new OceanX bank details. Is it genuine?

Treat it as fraud until verified. OceanX bank details are only published on OX Navigator and signed invoices. Call +65 6555 0188.
