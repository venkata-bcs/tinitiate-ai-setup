## 1. Overview
OceanX invoices through the local group entity in the country where freight or charges are payable. Each entity collects in the local currency where regulations require it, and in USD for international ocean freight where permitted. Every invoice shows the invoicing entity, tax registration number, Bill of Lading (B/L) number, booking number, currency and due date.

## 2. Invoicing rules
- **Freight invoices** are issued on vessel departure for prepaid freight, and on vessel arrival for collect freight.
- **Local charges** (terminal handling, documentation, delivery order fees) are invoiced by the origin or destination office.
- **Demurrage and detention** are invoiced when the container is returned (see OX-CUS-005).
- E-invoices are delivered through OX Navigator and by email. Paper invoices are available on request for USD 15 per invoice.
- Invoice disputes must be raised within **30 days** of the invoice date (see OX-CUS-003). The undisputed portion remains payable.

## 4. Payment terms
| Account tier | Terms | Late payment |
|---|---|---|
| Spot | Prepayment before B/L release or delivery order | Not applicable |
| Contract | Net 30 days from invoice date | 1.5% per month (or the local statutory maximum) |
| Partner (NVOCC) | Net 45 days | 1.5% per month |
| Strategic | As per service contract | As per contract |

**EU customers:** the statutory rate under Directive 2011/7/EU (ECB reference rate + 8 points) applies to late payments, plus a fixed EUR 40 recovery fee.
**India:** late payments by MSME-registered vendors are handled in line with the MSMED Act, where applicable.

## 5. Taxes

| Region | Tax treatment on ocean freight & local charges |
|---|---|
| China | VAT at 6% on international freight agency services; 9% on certain domestic transport. Fapiao issued on request within 5 working days. |
| USA | Ocean freight is generally not subject to sales tax. Some local services (warehousing, drayage) may be taxable depending on the state. |
| EU | International transport is zero-rated. Domestic services are charged at local VAT rates (e.g. 21% NL). Reverse charge applies for B2B cross-border services. |
| India | GST at 5% (with ITC conditions) or 18% depending on the service; IGST on import freight per notification. The customer's GSTIN must be on file for tax credit. |
| Singapore | GST at 9% on local services. International services are zero-rated under Section 21(3). |

## 6. Currency conversion
- USD-denominated charges paid in local currency are converted at the **OceanX weekly exchange rate**, published every Monday 00:00 UTC on OX Navigator.
- The rate used is the one in force on the invoice date, or on the vessel sailing date for prepaid ocean freight.
- Bank charges are borne by the payer (SWIFT "OUR"). Short payments due to bank fees above USD 25 are re-invoiced.

## 7. Refunds of overpayments
Overpayments are kept as a credit on account unless the customer asks for a refund. Refunds are made only to the original payer account, within 15 business days of a verified request. See OX-CUS-003.

## 8. Payment fraud prevention
- OceanX bank details are published only on OX Navigator and on signed invoices.
- Any message asking you to pay a "new" or "updated" account is suspicious. Verify by phone with a known contact before paying.
- Report suspected fraud to fraud@oceanxlogistics.example or +65 6555 0188 (24/7).
