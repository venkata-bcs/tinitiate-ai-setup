## 1. Policy statement
OceanX Logistics is committed to:
- the safety of life at sea
- preventing injury and loss of life
- protecting the marine environment
- protecting the property of our customers and our company

We comply with all applicable international conventions, flag-state and port-state requirements, and set our own standards higher where reasonably practicable.

## 3. Safety Management System (ISM Code)
- Every vessel holds a valid **Safety Management Certificate** and every operating entity a **Document of Compliance**.
- The **Designated Person Ashore (DPA)** has direct access to the Group CEO and can be reached 24/7 on +65 6555 0900.
- The Master has **overriding authority** to make decisions on safety and pollution prevention, and to request company assistance.
- Internal ISM audits are held every 12 months per vessel; management review is held twice a year.
- Near-miss reporting target: at least 4 reports per vessel per month. Reports are non-punitive.

## 4. Maintenance and dry-docking
- A Planned Maintenance System (PMS) is in place for all critical equipment. Overdue critical jobs are escalated to the Fleet Superintendent within 24 hours.
- Dry-docking takes place every 5 years, with an intermediate in-water survey at 2.5 years (class approval required).
- Hull cleaning and propeller polishing are scheduled on performance data to reduce fuel use.

## 5. Ship and port security (ISPS Code)
- Each vessel has an approved Ship Security Plan and a trained Ship Security Officer. The Company Security Officer is reachable 24/7.
- High-risk areas (Gulf of Aden, Red Sea, Gulf of Guinea, Sulu-Celebes Sea) need a voyage-specific risk assessment. Best Management Practices (BMP5 / BMP West Africa) are applied.
- Armed guards are used only where flag-state law allows it and the Security Committee approves.
- Stowaway, drug-smuggling and cyber threats are covered in the security plan and in annual drills.

## 6. Environmental protection

### 6.1 MARPOL compliance
- **Annex I (oil):** oily water separators are sealed and monitored; Oil Record Book entries are audited.
- **Annex V (garbage):** plastics are never discharged; all waste is segregated and landed ashore.
- **Annex VI (air):** sulphur cap of 0.50% globally and 0.10% in Emission Control Areas (North America, Baltic, North Sea, Mediterranean from May 2025).
- **Ballast Water Management Convention:** type-approved BWMS fitted on 100% of owned vessels.

### 6.2 Decarbonisation roadmap

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| 2030 | -40% carbon intensity (gCO2/TEU-km) vs 2008; 20% of fleet capable of zero-carbon fuel |
| 2040 | -70% absolute GHG emissions vs 2008 |
| 2050 | Net-zero across owned fleet (IMO 2023 Revised GHG Strategy) |

All vessels hold a valid EEXI certificate and an approved SEEMP Part III. Ships rated D for CII for two consecutive years, or E in any year, receive a corrective action plan within 3 months.

### 6.3 EU ETS and FuelEU Maritime
OceanX reports emissions under EU MRV and surrenders EU Allowances for voyages to and from EU ports. FuelEU Maritime compliance (2% GHG intensity reduction from 2025) is managed across the fleet through pooling.

## 7. Port State Control readiness
A pre-arrival checklist is completed 72 hours before arrival at ports with targeted inspection regimes (Paris MoU, Tokyo MoU, USCG). Any deficiency is closed out with root-cause analysis within 30 days.

## 8. Emergency preparedness
- Shipboard drills: fire and abandon ship monthly; enclosed space entry every 2 months; SOPEP oil spill drill every 3 months.
- A shore-based Emergency Response Team exercise with a simulated casualty is held twice a year.
- 24/7 Emergency Response Centre: +65 6555 0911.

## 9. Responsibilities
The Group CEO is ultimately accountable for this policy. The DPA and Fleet Directors implement it. Every seafarer and shore employee has the authority and duty to **stop work** if they believe it is unsafe.
