## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how OceanX Logistics ("OceanX", "we") opens customer accounts. Our checks protect customers and OceanX from fraud, sanctions breaches, money laundering and false cargo declarations. They also help us meet maritime security and trade laws in every country we serve.

## 2. Who needs an account
Anyone who books cargo, pays freight or receives a Bill of Lading from OceanX must hold a verified **OX Customer Account**. Notify parties who only receive arrival notices may be registered as *contacts* without a full account.

| Customer type | Account tier | Typical credit |
|---|---|---|
| Spot shipper (occasional) | Spot | Prepaid only |
| Contract shipper (service contract / NAC) | Contract | Up to 30 days |
| Freight forwarder / NVOCC | Partner | Up to 45 days |
| Key account (> 5,000 TEU / year) | Strategic | Negotiated |

## 3. Documents required

### 3.1 All business customers
- Certificate of incorporation or business registration (no older than 12 months)
- Tax ID: EIN (USA), VAT number (EU), GSTIN (India), USCC (China), UEN (Singapore)
- Registered and operating address (proof dated within 3 months)
- List of directors, and of beneficial owners holding 25% or more
- Photo ID of the authorised signatory
- Signed OceanX Customer Terms and the Data Processing Notice

### 3.2 Additional documents by region

| Region | Additional requirement |
|---|---|
| USA | FMC licence or bond number for NVOCCs; CBP importer number for importers of record |
| EU | EORI number; AEO certificate, if held |
| China | Import & export operator record; 10-digit customs registration code |
| India | Importer Exporter Code (IEC); AD code registration with the port |
| Singapore | Customs Central Registration (CR) number |

### 3.3 Freight forwarders and NVOCCs
Forwarders must also provide evidence of liability insurance (minimum USD 1 million per occurrence). Where applicable, they must also show FIATA or local association membership.

## 4. Verification checks
OceanX performs the following checks before activating an account:

1. **Identity & registry check:** company registry lookup and a review of document authenticity.
2. **Sanctions & denied-party screening:** against UN, OFAC SDN, EU, UK HMT, Singapore MAS and China export control lists. Active accounts are re-screened daily.
3. **Ownership check:** beneficial owners are screened for sanctions and for politically exposed person (PEP) status.
4. **Trade-pattern risk review:** high-risk commodities and high-risk destinations require Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD). High-risk commodities include dual-use items, scrap metal, used vehicles and chemicals.
5. **Credit assessment:** for the Contract, Partner and Strategic tiers, using two years of financial statements and a trade-credit bureau report.

> Accounts with a confirmed sanctions match are declined and may be reported to the authorities. Where the law forbids us to disclose screening results, OceanX will not explain them.

## 5. Timelines

| Step | Standard | Enhanced Due Diligence |
|---|---|---|
| Document review | 1 business day | 2 business days |
| Screening & risk scoring | Same day | 3 - 5 business days |
| Credit decision | 3 business days | 5 business days |
| Portal access (OX Navigator) | Within 24 h of approval | Within 24 h of approval |

## 6. Credit terms
- Credit limits are set in USD and reviewed every 12 months, or sooner after two late payments.
- Security, such as a bank guarantee or standby letter of credit, may be required for companies trading for less than 2 years.
- Credit can be suspended immediately if invoices are more than 30 days overdue. Cargo is then released only after payment ("cash against release").

## 7. Portal access - OX Navigator
Each account has one **Account Administrator**, who creates users and assigns roles (Booker, Documentation, Finance, Viewer). Multi-factor authentication is mandatory. Users inactive for 180 days are disabled automatically.

## 8. Keeping information current
Customers must report changes in ownership, address, bank details or authorised signatories within 15 days. OceanX re-verifies accounts on this cycle:
- Spot and Contract accounts: every 3 years
- Partner accounts: every 2 years
- EDD accounts: every year

> **Fraud warning:** OceanX will never ask you to change bank details by email alone. Any change to OceanX's payment details is confirmed by a signed letter on the OX Navigator portal. Call your account manager before paying to new details.
