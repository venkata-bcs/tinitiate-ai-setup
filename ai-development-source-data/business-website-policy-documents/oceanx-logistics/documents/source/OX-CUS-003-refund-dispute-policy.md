## 1. Purpose
OceanX aims to invoice correctly the first time. If something goes wrong, this policy explains how to dispute a charge, when you are entitled to a refund, and how disagreements are escalated.

## 2. What can be disputed

| Dispute type | Examples | Deadline to raise |
|---|---|---|
| Rate dispute | Rate differs from quotation or service contract | 30 days from invoice |
| Quantity / equipment | Wrong container count, size or type | 30 days from invoice |
| Duplicate billing | Same charge invoiced twice | 90 days from invoice |
| Demurrage & detention | Free time miscalculated; delay caused by the carrier | 30 days from invoice |
| Surcharge applicability | BAF/PSS applied outside its validity period | 30 days from invoice |
| Service failure | Rolled booking, missed transhipment, late equipment release | 60 days from event |

Cargo loss or damage is **not** handled under this policy. File a cargo claim under OX-CUS-007 Cargo Claims Policy.

## 3. How to raise a dispute
1. Log in to **OX Navigator > Billing > Dispute** and select the invoice line(s).
2. State the reason and the amount in dispute, and attach evidence (quotation, contract amendment, EIR, gate-in record).
3. You receive a case number immediately (format: `DSP-YYYY-NNNNNN`).
4. Pay the **undisputed** portion by the due date. Disputed amounts are put on hold from collection and late-payment interest.

## 4. Resolution timelines

| Stage | Who | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Billing Desk | 1 business day |
| Investigation & decision (standard) | Billing Desk | 10 business days |
| Investigation & decision (complex, multi-party) | Regional Finance | 25 business days |
| Credit note issued after approval | Billing Desk | 3 business days |

If a dispute is approved, OceanX issues a **credit note** against the original invoice. Credit notes may be applied to open invoices or refunded.

## 5. Refunds

### 5.1 Eligible refunds
- Overpayments and duplicate payments
- Approved disputes where the invoice was already paid
- Cancelled bookings where freight was prepaid (minus any cancellation fee under the Booking & Cancellation Policy)
- Container deposits (where applicable, e.g. India and China) after the equipment is returned in good condition

### 5.2 Refund process
- Refunds are paid to the **original paying account only**, in the original currency.
- A signed refund request on company letterhead is required for amounts above USD 10,000.
- Target payment: **15 business days** after approval (30 business days for China, due to SAFE documentation).
- Container deposit refunds in India: within 21 days of the EIR showing a clean return.

### 5.3 Non-refundable charges
Documentation fees for issued B/Ls, amendment fees after B/L issue, and government fees paid on the customer's behalf (customs duties, port dues) are not refundable by OceanX.

## 6. Escalation
If you are not satisfied with a dispute decision:

1. **Level 1 - Regional Finance Manager:** request a review within 15 days of the decision. Response within 10 business days.
2. **Level 2 - Customer Experience Ombudsperson** (ombuds@oceanxlogistics.example): an independent internal review. Response within 20 business days.
3. **Level 3 - Formal dispute resolution:** under the governing law and forum in the B/L or service contract. For US trades, customers may also contact the Federal Maritime Commission's Office of Consumer Affairs & Dispute Resolution Services (CADRS).

## 7. Demurrage & detention disputes (US trades)
In line with the US Ocean Shipping Reform Act 2022 (OSRA 2022) and 46 CFR Part 541, OceanX's US demurrage and detention invoices include all required information. They are issued within 30 days of the last charge, and disputes can be raised for at least 30 days after the invoice. Invoices that do not meet these requirements need not be paid.

## 8. Records
All disputes are logged and retained for 7 years. Monthly dispute statistics (volume, root cause, cycle time) are reviewed by the Commercial Excellence Committee to fix recurring billing errors.
