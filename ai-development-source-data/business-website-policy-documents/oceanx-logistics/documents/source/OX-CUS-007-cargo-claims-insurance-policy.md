## 1. Notice of loss or damage
- **Visible damage:** note it on the delivery receipt / Equipment Interchange Receipt (EIR) **at the time of delivery**, and notify OceanX in writing.
- **Concealed damage:** notify OceanX in writing within **3 days** of delivery (Hague-Visby Rules, Art. III r.6).
- **Total loss / non-delivery:** notify OceanX within **60 days** of the expected delivery date.
- Submit claims online: OX Navigator > Claims, or claims@oceanxlogistics.example.

## 2. Documents to support a claim
1. Original or copy B/L / Sea Waybill
2. Commercial invoice and packing list
3. Survey report (OceanX may appoint a joint surveyor; please allow access before cargo is disposed of)
4. Photos of the container, seal, packaging and damaged goods
5. Delivery receipt or EIR with remarks
6. Repair estimate or salvage sale proceeds
7. Statement of claim with calculation

## 3. Time bar
Legal proceedings must be started within **1 year** of delivery (or of the date the goods should have been delivered) under the Hague-Visby Rules and COGSA. Extensions must be agreed in writing **before** the time bar expires.

## 4. Carrier liability limits

| Regime | Applies to | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Hague-Visby Rules | Most non-US trades | 666.67 SDR per package or 2 SDR per kg gross, whichever is higher |
| US COGSA 1936 | Shipments to/from the USA | USD 500 per package or customary freight unit |
| China Maritime Code | China domestic jurisdiction | 666.67 SDR per package or 2 SDR per kg |
| Declared value (ad valorem) | Value declared on B/L and extra freight paid | Declared value |

The carrier is not liable for losses caused by the excepted perils in the B/L. These include insufficient packing, inherent vice, the shipper's own stuffing (Shipper's Load, Stow & Count), and perils of the sea.

## 5. General average
If general average is declared (for example, after a fire, grounding or salvage), cargo owners must provide security before the cargo is released. This means a GA bond and a GA guarantee from an insurer, or a cash deposit. **Cargo insurance is strongly recommended**, because uninsured cargo owners must pay these deposits themselves.

## 6. OceanX Marine Cover (optional cargo insurance)
Cover is arranged through a panel of A-rated marine insurers (policy administered by OceanX Marine Cover Pte. Ltd., fictional).

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Coverage basis | Institute Cargo Clauses (A) - "all risks" |
| Insured value | CIF value + 10% |
| Premium | From 0.18% of insured value (min USD 25 per shipment) |
| Deductible | USD 250 per claim (reefer: USD 1,000) |
| Excluded | Delay, inherent vice, insufficient packing, sanctions-restricted voyages |
| Claims settlement target | 30 days after a complete file is submitted |

Insurance must be bought **before** the vessel departs, through OX Navigator > Add-ons > Marine Cover.
