## 1. Principle
The **shipper and the consignee** are responsible for accurate customs declarations, correct classification (HS codes), valuation, origin and payment of duties and taxes. OceanX, as carrier, must submit advance cargo information to customs authorities using the data the shipper provides. Inaccurate or late data can lead to holds, fines, no-load orders or seizure. These costs are charged to the cargo interests.

## 2. Documents required for every shipment
- Commercial invoice (seller, buyer, Incoterms 2020 rule, currency, unit price, total value)
- Packing list (packages, gross and net weight, dimensions, marks and numbers)
- Shipping instructions (SI) submitted through OX Navigator
- Verified Gross Mass (VGM) under SOLAS Chapter VI, Regulation 2
- Certificate of origin, where preferential duty is claimed
- Licences or permits for controlled goods (dual-use, CITES, agricultural, pharmaceuticals)
- Dangerous Goods Declaration and Container Packing Certificate for IMDG cargo (OX-CUS-006)

## 3. Advance filing deadlines

| Destination / route | Filing | Deadline | Filed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| USA | AMS / 24-Hour Rule | 24 h before loading at foreign port | Carrier (and NVOCC for house B/Ls) |
| USA | ISF "10+2" | 24 h before loading | Importer (10), carrier (2) |
| Canada | ACI eManifest | 24 h before loading | Carrier |
| European Union | ICS2 Release 3 ENS | 24 h before loading (deep sea) | Carrier + house-level filers |
| China | China Advance Manifest | 24 h before loading | Carrier; consignee and shipper must include USCC/AEO code |
| India | IGM / Advance Arrival Manifest | Before vessel arrival (ICEGATE) | Carrier / shipping line agent |
| Singapore | TradeNet declaration | Before cargo release (import) or loading (export) | Declaring agent |
| Japan | AFR | 24 h before loading | Carrier / NVOCC |

**OceanX SI cut-off** is 48 hours before vessel ETD for all US, Canada, EU and China-bound cargo. Late SIs incur a **USD 75 late-documentation fee** and may cause the container to be rolled.

## 4. Cargo description rules
- Descriptions must be precise. "Freight of all kinds", "general cargo", "consolidated" and "said to contain" without detail are rejected.
- At least a 6-digit HS code is required for all destinations; 8 digits are required for China and India, and 10 digits are recommended for the EU.
- Weights must match the VGM to within 5%.

## 6. Incoterms and responsibility for duties
OceanX follows the Incoterms 2020 rule stated on the commercial invoice to determine who pays origin and destination charges. If it is unclear, the **consignee** is billed for destination charges and the **shipper** for origin charges.

| Incoterm | Export clearance | Import clearance & duties |
|---|---|---|
| EXW | Buyer | Buyer |
| FCA / FOB | Seller | Buyer |
| CFR / CIF / CPT / CIP | Seller | Buyer |
| DAP / DPU | Seller | Buyer |
| DDP | Seller | **Seller** |

## 7. Holds, examinations and penalties
- Customs holds and examinations are notified through OX Navigator and by email within 2 hours of OceanX becoming aware of them.
- Examination, devanning, storage and demurrage costs during a hold are charged to the cargo interests.
- Fines imposed on OceanX for inaccurate shipper data (e.g. a US CBP liquidated damages claim or an EU ICS2 penalty) are recharged at cost, plus a USD 250 administration fee.

## 8. Sanctions and export controls
OceanX will not carry cargo that would breach UN, US, EU, UK, Singapore or Chinese sanctions or export controls. We screen all parties and selected HS codes. Bookings may be held or cancelled without liability if screening identifies a risk.

## 9. Record keeping
Customs documents are kept for at least 5 years (USA: 5 years under 19 CFR 163; EU: 3 - 10 years by member state; India: 5 years; China: 3 years; Singapore: 5 years).
