## 1. Welcome and purpose

Welcome to OceanX Logistics. Since 1998 we have grown from a two-vessel feeder operator in Singapore into a global carrier serving 214 ports from 46 countries. This handbook explains what you can expect from OceanX and what OceanX expects from you. Country supplements (Annex A) apply where local law gives employees greater rights. If the two conflict, the more favourable provision applies.

## 2. Our values - "SAIL"

| Value | What it means in practice |

## 3. Employment basics

### 3.1 Recruitment and onboarding
- All vacancies are advertised internally for at least five working days before external release, except for confidential senior roles.
- Offers are conditional on reference checks, right-to-work verification and, for finance, compliance and IT roles, a background screening by our vendor ClearPath Screening (fictional).
- New joiners complete the 30-day "First Voyage" onboarding programme: mandatory e-learning (Code of Conduct, Anti-Bribery, Sanctions, Data Privacy, Information Security), a buddy assignment, and a port or vessel visit within the first 90 days.

### 3.2 Probation
Probation is three months (six months for managerial grades M3 and above). Managers must hold a documented review at the mid-point and at the end of probation.

### 3.3 Offboarding
Leavers return all equipment, ID badges and physical documents on the last working day. IT access is revoked by 23:59 local time on the last day. An exit interview is offered to every leaver.

## 5. Hybrid working

Eligible roles may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Working remotely from a country other than your country of employment needs prior written approval from HR and Tax, because it can create tax ("permanent establishment") and immigration risks. Customer-facing operations desks and the Global Operations Centre are excluded from hybrid working.

## 6. Pay, benefits and expenses
- Salaries are reviewed annually in March. The variable bonus plan is linked 60% to company results and 40% to individual objectives.
- Group benefits include medical insurance, life and disability cover, an employee assistance programme (24/7 counselling) and a retirement plan matched to local practice.
- Business travel must be booked through the corporate travel tool. Economy class applies to flights under 6 hours and premium economy to longer flights. Business class is allowed only for flights over 9 hours, with EVP approval.
- Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with itemised receipts.

## 7. Code of conduct

### 7.1 Respect and equal opportunity
OceanX does not tolerate discrimination, harassment or bullying on the basis of race, colour, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, nationality, marital status or any other protected characteristic. This applies at the office, in ports, on vessels, at company events and online.

### 7.2 Conflicts of interest
Declare any relationship with a customer, supplier, agent or competitor that could influence, or appear to influence, your decisions. Declarations are made in the Compliance Portal within 14 days.

### 7.3 Gifts and hospitality

| Item | Threshold (USD equivalent) | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Gifts given or received | up to 100 | Allowed; record in the register |
| Gifts given or received | 100 - 300 | Line manager approval before acceptance |
| Gifts given or received | over 300 | Compliance approval; usually declined |
| Any gift to a public official (customs, port authority, coast guard) | any value | Prohibited without Compliance pre-approval |
| Cash or cash equivalents | any value | Always prohibited |

Facilitation payments ("speed money") to customs or port officials are prohibited in all countries, even where local practice tolerates them. See the Group Anti-Bribery & Sanctions Policy.

### 7.4 Confidentiality and data
Customer shipment data, freight rates and contract terms are confidential. Personal data is processed only in line with the Group Data Privacy Policy, which covers GDPR, PDPA Singapore, PIPL China, DPDP India and US state laws.

### 7.5 Competition law
Never discuss prices, surcharges, capacity plans or customers with competitors outside an approved consortium or alliance meeting attended by a lawyer.

## 8. Performance and development
Annual objectives are set in January, checked in July and reviewed at year end in December. Ratings use a five-point scale and are calibrated across teams. Every employee has access to the OceanX Academy, which offers 40+ courses in shipping operations, customs, finance and leadership.

## 9. Disciplinary and grievance procedure
1. **Informal resolution:** most issues should first be raised with the line manager.
2. **Formal grievance:** submitted in writing to HR. HR acknowledges it within 3 working days and investigates within 20 working days.
3. **Disciplinary stages:** verbal warning, written warning, final written warning and dismissal. Gross misconduct may lead to immediate dismissal. Examples are theft, fraud, violence, bribery, serious safety breaches and working under the influence.
4. **Appeal:** within 10 working days, to a manager not previously involved.

## 10. Speaking up
Concerns can be raised confidentially or anonymously through the **OceanX SpeakUp line** (web or phone, available in 14 languages, run by an independent provider). Retaliating against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary offence.
