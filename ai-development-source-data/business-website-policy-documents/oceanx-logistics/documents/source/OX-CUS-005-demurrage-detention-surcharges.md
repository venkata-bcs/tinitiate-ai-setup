## 1. Definitions
- **Demurrage:** a charge for a full container staying inside the terminal beyond its free time (import: after discharge; export: before loading).
- **Detention:** a charge for using OceanX equipment outside the terminal beyond its free time (import: from gate-out full to gate-in empty; export: from gate-out empty to gate-in full).
- **Combined (merged) free time:** where shown below, demurrage and detention free days are pooled into a single allowance.
- **Free time** starts at 00:01 on the day after discharge (import) or empty pick-up (export), and is counted in calendar days including weekends and public holidays, unless local regulations say otherwise.

## 2. Standard free time

| Region | Import demurrage | Import detention | Export (combined) |
|---|---|---|---|
| USA (West Coast) | 4 days | 4 days | 7 days |
| USA (East / Gulf Coast) | 5 days | 4 days | 7 days |
| Northern Europe | 5 days | 5 days | 7 days |
| Mediterranean | 6 days | 6 days | 7 days |
| China | 7 days (combined) | - | 7 days |
| India | 7 days (combined) | - | 10 days |
| Singapore | 5 days | 5 days | 5 days |

Reefer and special equipment (open top, flat rack) receive **half** the standard free time, rounded down.

## 3. Daily rates after free time (per container, USD)

| Period after free time | 20' Dry | 40' / 40'HC Dry | 20' / 40' Reefer | Special equipment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1 - 4 | 95 | 150 | 220 | 210 |
| Days 5 - 10 | 160 | 260 | 360 | 340 |
| Day 11 onwards | 240 | 380 | 480 | 460 |

Local currency equivalents apply in China (CNY), India (INR), the EU (EUR) and Singapore (SGD). They are calculated at the OceanX weekly exchange rate (OX-CUS-002).

## 4. Fair-charging principles
OceanX applies demurrage and detention to encourage prompt cargo pick-up and equipment return, not as a source of profit. We will **waive or suspend** charges in these cases:

- Terminal closure, strike, or a port authority embargo that prevents pick-up or return
- Delays caused by OceanX or our contracted terminal or haulier (e.g. equipment not available, vessel bunching)
- No appointment slots available at the terminal for return, if documented with a screenshot or terminal record
- Government holds where the customs exam finds no discrepancy (USA, under 46 CFR 545.5 guidance)

Waivers are requested through OX Navigator > Billing > Dispute (OX-CUS-003).

## 5. Common surcharges

| Code | Name | Basis | Revision |
|---|---|---|---|
| BAF | Bunker Adjustment Factor | Per TEU, by trade lane; linked to the VLSFO 0.5% Singapore/Rotterdam average | Quarterly |
| LSS | Low Sulphur Surcharge | Included in BAF for most trades since 2025 | Quarterly |
| EUA | EU ETS Surcharge | Per TEU on voyages to/from the EU (EU Emissions Trading System) | Quarterly |
| CAF | Currency Adjustment Factor | % of base freight on trades invoiced in volatile currencies | Monthly |
| THC / OTHC / DTHC | Terminal Handling Charge (origin / destination) | Per container, passed through at port tariff | As published |
| PSS | Peak Season Surcharge | Per container, during declared peak periods | With 30 days' notice |
| GRI | General Rate Increase | Per container, trade-wide | With 30 days' notice (US FMC tariffs) |
| CIC | Container Imbalance Charge | Per container where equipment must be repositioned | Quarterly |
| WRS | War Risk Surcharge | Per container for high-risk areas (e.g. Red Sea / Gulf of Aden) | As required |
| DOC | Documentation Fee | Per B/L | Annual |
| ISPS | Port Security Charge | Per container | As published |

US-trade surcharges and rate increases are published in the OceanX FMC tariff at least **30 days** before they take effect (exceptions apply under 46 CFR 520).

## 7. Example calculation
A 40'HC dry import container is discharged at Long Beach on 2 March and picked up on 9 March (4 free days):
- Free time: 3 - 6 March (4 days)
- Chargeable: 7, 8, 9 March = 3 days x USD 150 = **USD 450 demurrage**
