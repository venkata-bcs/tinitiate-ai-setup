## 1. Why this matters
Undeclared or misdeclared dangerous goods are a leading cause of container-ship fires. One misdeclared container can endanger the crew, the vessel and thousands of other customers' cargo. OceanX applies a **zero-tolerance** approach to misdeclaration.

## 2. Legal framework
- International Maritime Dangerous Goods (IMDG) Code, Amendment 42-24 (mandatory from 1 January 2026)
- SOLAS Chapter VII; MARPOL Annex III
- US 49 CFR Parts 171-180 (Hazardous Materials Regulations) for US inland legs
- ADR/RID for European inland legs
- National port regulations (e.g. MPA Singapore Port Regulations, China MSA rules, Indian DG Shipping circulars)

## 3. Booking dangerous goods
1. Request DG approval in **OX Navigator > Bookings > Hazardous** at least **7 days** before vessel cut-off (**14 days** for Class 1 and Class 7).
2. Provide the UN number, proper shipping name, class, packing group, flash point, net and gross quantity, packaging type, marine pollutant status, and emergency contact (24/7).
3. Upload the Safety Data Sheet (SDS, issued within 5 years) and a multimodal Dangerous Goods Declaration (DGD).
4. Following OceanX DG Desk approval, upload the Container Packing Certificate signed by a trained packer.
5. Approval is valid only for the specific booking and vessel. Rolled bookings need re-approval.

## 4. Acceptance by class

| IMDG Class | Description | OceanX acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Explosives | Division 1.4S only; others **not accepted** |
| 2.1 | Flammable gases | Accepted, with restrictions on certain trades |
| 2.2 / 2.3 | Non-flammable / toxic gases | 2.2 accepted; 2.3 case by case |
| 3 | Flammable liquids | Accepted |
| 4.1 / 4.2 / 4.3 | Flammable solids; spontaneously combustible; dangerous when wet | Accepted with restrictions; **calcium hypochlorite (UN 1748, 2208, 2880) needs special approval** |
| 5.1 / 5.2 | Oxidisers; organic peroxides | 5.1 accepted; 5.2 temperature-controlled only, case by case |
| 6.1 / 6.2 | Toxic; infectious substances | 6.1 accepted; 6.2 **not accepted** (except UN 3373 Category B) |
| 7 | Radioactive | **Not accepted** |
| 8 | Corrosives | Accepted |
| 9 | Miscellaneous (incl. lithium batteries UN 3480/3481/3090/3091, vehicles UN 3171) | Accepted with lithium battery declaration |

## 5. Lithium batteries and electric vehicles
- State of charge must not exceed **30%** for UN 3480 batteries shipped on their own, and **50%** for battery-electric vehicles.
- Damaged, defective or recalled batteries are **not accepted**.
- Electric vehicles must be declared as UN 3171. Used EVs need a battery inspection certificate issued no more than 90 days before shipment.

## 6. Prohibited items (never accepted)
- Weapons, ammunition and military goods (except Class 1.4S sporting cartridges with permits)
- Radioactive material (Class 7)
- Narcotics, illegal drugs and psychotropic substances
- Counterfeit goods and goods that infringe intellectual property
- Hazardous waste under the Basel Convention without written consent
- Ivory, rhino horn and CITES Appendix I species
- Human remains (other than cremated remains with permits)
- Any goods destined for a sanctioned party, or prohibited under applicable sanctions
- Charcoal and seed cake that are not certified to IMDG Special Provision 925/955 rules

## 7. Restricted items (prior approval required)
- Scrap metal and used tyres (risk of hidden waste and contaminants)
- Used vehicles and machinery (must be drained, with batteries disconnected)
- Personal effects and household goods (no DG allowed inside)
- Pharmaceuticals and high-value electronics (security seal and GPS tracker recommended)
- Live animals, plants and soil (need phytosanitary and veterinary certificates)

## 8. Misdeclaration penalties

| Situation | Penalty (per container) |
|---|---|
| Undeclared or misdeclared dangerous goods | **USD 35,000**, plus all costs, fines and claims |
| Undeclared lithium batteries | USD 15,000, plus costs |
| Incorrect weight (VGM varies by more than 5%) | USD 1,500 |
| Missing or invalid DG documents at gate-in | Container refused; USD 500 re-handling fee |

OceanX may inspect containers at any time without notice to verify declarations. Repeat offenders' accounts are terminated and reported to the relevant authorities.
