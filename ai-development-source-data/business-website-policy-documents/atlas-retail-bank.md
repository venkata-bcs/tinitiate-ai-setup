## Explainer

Atlas Retail Bank, N.A. was founded in Baltimore in 1927 as a mutual savings bank. It is now a **USD 48.6 billion** national bank with **162 branches** in 13 states plus DC (Maine to Florida). The bank serves 2.3 million consumers and 185,000 small businesses. Its policies reflect the dense US consumer-banking rulebook:
- **Customer Identification Program** (PATRIOT Act)
- **Truth in Savings** (Reg DD) and **Funds Availability** (Reg CC)
- **Electronic Fund Transfers** (Reg E)
- **GLBA privacy**, **BSA/AML & OFAC**
- **ECOA / Fair Housing / HMDA / CRA**
- state rules from NY, NJ, MA, MD and others
