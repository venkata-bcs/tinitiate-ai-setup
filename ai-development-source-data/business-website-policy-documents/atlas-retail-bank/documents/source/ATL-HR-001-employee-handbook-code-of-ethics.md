## 1. Our purpose and values
Atlas Retail Bank has served communities from Maine to Florida since 1927. Our purpose is **to help families and small businesses on the East Coast build financial strength**. Our values are:
- **Integrity:** do the right thing, especially when no one is watching.
- **Customer first:** recommend only what genuinely meets the customer's needs.
- **Respect:** value every colleague and customer.
- **Stewardship:** protect the bank's safety and soundness and our customers' trust.

## 2. Employment eligibility and screening
- All offers depend on a background check, credit check (where permitted by state law) and **fingerprinting under Section 19 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act**. Anyone convicted of a crime involving dishonesty, breach of trust or money laundering cannot be employed without FDIC consent.
- Credit history is not used for hiring in states and cities that restrict it (e.g. New York City, Connecticut, Maryland, Washington DC), except where a legal exemption applies.
- Form I-9 employment eligibility is verified within 3 business days of hire.
- Loan originators are registered with the NMLS before taking any mortgage applications.

## 3. Equal employment opportunity
Atlas is an equal opportunity employer. We make decisions about employment without regard to:
- race, colour, religion or creed
- sex (including pregnancy), sexual orientation or gender identity
- national origin, age, disability or genetic information
- veteran status, marital status
- any other characteristic protected by federal, state or local law

Reasonable accommodations are provided for disability, pregnancy and religious practice.

## 5. Leave and time off
- **Paid time off:** 18 days per year in years 1-4, 23 days in years 5-9, and 28 days from year 10.
- **Bank holidays:** the 11 Federal Reserve holidays.
- **Sick leave:** at least the amount required by state or local law (e.g. NY Paid Sick Leave, NJ Earned Sick Leave, MD Healthy Working Families Act). Atlas standard is 7 days.
- **Parental leave:** 16 weeks fully paid for birth, adoption or foster placement.
- **FMLA** and state family-leave programmes (NY PFL, NJ FLI, MA PFML, CT PFML, MD FAMLI) are coordinated with Atlas pay.
- **Mandatory consecutive leave:** associates in sensitive roles (cash, wires, lending approvals, IT administration) must take **10 consecutive business days** off each year. This is a fraud-prevention control.

## 6. Code of ethics

### 6.1 Sales practices
- Accounts or products may only be opened with the customer's **informed consent**, documented in the system.
- Atlas does not set product sales quotas for branch associates. Incentives are balanced across customer satisfaction, compliance and quality, and are clawed back if misconduct is found.
- Opening unauthorised accounts, inflating sales or pressuring customers is grounds for termination and may be reported to regulators.

### 6.2 Conflicts of interest
- Associates may not process transactions on their own accounts, or on the accounts of family members or close associates.
- Outside employment and board seats need pre-approval from Compliance.
- Loans to executive officers and directors follow **Regulation O** limits and Board approval rules.

### 6.3 Gifts and entertainment
Gifts from customers or vendors may not exceed **USD 100 per year** per source. The **Bank Bribery Act** (18 U.S.C. 215) makes it a federal crime to corruptly accept anything of value in connection with bank business. Report any offer above the limit to Compliance.

### 6.4 Personal trading
Associates with access to material non-public information must pre-clear securities trades, and may not trade in Atlas Financial Holdings stock during blackout periods.

### 6.5 Confidentiality
Customer information is protected under the **Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act**. Access only what you need for your job. Browsing celebrity or colleague accounts is prohibited and monitored.

## 8. Performance, discipline and separation
- Performance reviews are held mid-year and at year-end. Compliance behaviour is a "gate": a serious compliance failure caps the rating.
- Corrective action steps are coaching, a written warning, a final warning and termination. Fraud, theft and wilful policy breaches may result in immediate termination.
- On separation, system access is removed **the same business day**, and keys, badges and tokens are collected.

## 9. Ethics hotline and non-retaliation
- **Atlas EthicsLine:** 1-800-555-0119 (24/7, independent provider) or ethicsline.atlasretailbank.example
- Reports can be anonymous. Retaliation is prohibited and protected under Sarbanes-Oxley and Dodd-Frank whistleblower provisions.
- Associates may report concerns directly to regulators (OCC, CFPB, SEC) without notifying Atlas.
