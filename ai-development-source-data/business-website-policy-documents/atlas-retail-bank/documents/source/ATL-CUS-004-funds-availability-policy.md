## 1. Your ability to withdraw funds
Our policy is to make funds from your cash and check deposits available to you **on the first business day after the day we receive your deposit**. Electronic direct deposits are available on the day we receive them. Once funds are available, you can withdraw them in cash and we will use them to pay checks you have written.

For determining availability, every day is a business day except Saturdays, Sundays and federal holidays.

## 2. Deposit cut-off times

| Deposit method | Cut-off (Eastern Time) |
|---|---|
| Branch teller | Close of business (posted branch hours) |
| Atlas ATM | 9:00 pm |
| Mobile check deposit | 9:00 pm |
| Night depository | Treated as received the next business day |
| Non-Atlas ATM | Up to 5 business days (deposits not accepted at most networks) |

## 3. Same-day availability
These deposits are available on the **day of deposit**:
- cash deposited in person with a teller
- electronic direct deposits (payroll, Social Security) and incoming wire transfers
- transfers between Atlas accounts
- Zelle payments received

Atlas makes direct deposits available **up to 2 days early** when the payer sends the file early ("Early Pay"). This is not guaranteed.

## 4. Longer delays may apply
In some cases we will not make all the funds you deposit by check available on the first business day. Depending on the type of check, funds may not be available until the **second business day** after deposit. The first **USD 275** of your deposits, however, will be available on the first business day.

If we are not going to make all the funds from your deposit available on the first business day, we will tell you at the time of deposit. If we decide after the deposit, we will mail you a notice by the day after we receive it.

### 4.1 Exception holds - up to 7 business days
Funds may be delayed longer in these situations:
- we believe a check you deposit will not be paid
- you deposit checks totalling more than **USD 6,725** on any one day
- you redeposit a check that has been returned unpaid
- you have overdrawn your account repeatedly in the last six months
- there is an emergency, such as failure of computer or communications equipment

## 5. Special rules for new accounts
During the **first 30 days** the account is open:
- cash, wire and electronic direct deposits: available on the day we receive them
- the first USD 6,725 of a day's total deposits of cashier's, certified, teller's, traveller's and federal, state and local government checks: available on the first business day after deposit, if the deposit meets certain conditions
- the excess over USD 6,725 and all other checks: available on the **ninth business day** after deposit

## 6. Mobile deposit
- Funds from mobile check deposits made before 9:00 pm ET are generally available the next business day. The first USD 275 may be available immediately for customers with accounts open 90+ days.
- Mobile deposit limits: USD 5,000 per day and USD 15,000 per 30 days (Premier Checking: USD 25,000 / USD 50,000).
- Keep the paper check for 14 days, then destroy it securely.

## 7. Holds on other funds
If we cash a check for you that is drawn on another bank, we may withhold the same amount of funds already in your account. These funds become available at the time the cashed check would have become available had you deposited it.

*Dollar thresholds reflect the inflation adjustments to Regulation CC effective 1 July 2025.*
