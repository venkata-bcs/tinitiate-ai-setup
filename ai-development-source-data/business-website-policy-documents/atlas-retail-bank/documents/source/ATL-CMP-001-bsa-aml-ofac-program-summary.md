## 1. Program overview
Atlas maintains a Bank Secrecy Act / Anti-Money Laundering (BSA/AML) program reasonably designed to comply with the BSA (31 U.S.C. 5311 et seq.), the USA PATRIOT Act, the Anti-Money Laundering Act of 2020, OCC regulations (12 CFR 21.21) and FinCEN regulations (31 CFR Chapter X). Sanctions compliance follows OFAC regulations (31 CFR Chapter V).

The program has the required **five pillars**:

| Pillar | How Atlas meets it |
|---|---|
| 1. System of internal controls | Risk assessment, CIP/CDD/EDD, transaction monitoring, sanctions screening, reporting |
| 2. Independent testing | Annual audit by Internal Audit; triennial external model validation |
| 3. Designated BSA Officer | Raymond Castellano, reporting to the Chief Compliance Officer and the Board Risk Committee |
| 4. Training | Annual for all associates; enhanced modules for frontline, operations and lending |
| 5. Customer due diligence | Risk-based CDD, beneficial ownership and ongoing monitoring |

## 2. Risk assessment
The enterprise BSA/AML risk assessment is refreshed each year, and after any major change (new product, acquisition, new geography). It considers:
- **Products and services:** cash, wires, Zelle, ACH, remote deposit capture, prepaid cards, safe deposit boxes
- **Customers:** MSBs, cash-intensive businesses, third-party payment processors, non-resident aliens, PEPs
- **Geographies:** HIDTA/HIFCA areas (e.g. New York/New Jersey, Miami, Baltimore-Washington); high-risk countries for wires
- **Delivery channels:** branch, online, mobile, ATM

## 3. Customer due diligence
- CIP at account opening (see ATL-CUS-001).
- Beneficial ownership for legal-entity customers.
- Customer risk rating, recalculated on trigger events and at least every 12 months (high), 24 months (moderate) or 36 months (low).
- **Enhanced Due Diligence** for high-risk customers: source of funds and wealth, expected activity, site visits for cash-intensive businesses, and senior management approval for PEPs.
- **Prohibited relationships:** shell banks, unlicensed MSBs, online gambling operators without a valid state licence, marijuana-related businesses, and virtual-asset service providers without Board-approved risk appetite.

## 4. Transaction monitoring
- Automated monitoring of 120+ scenarios (structuring, rapid movement of funds, funnel accounts, elder exploitation patterns, human-trafficking typologies, check kiting).
- Alerts are reviewed within **30 days**. Cases are escalated to the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU).
- Model tuning is validated each year. Below-the-line testing is done every six months.

## 5. Regulatory reporting

| Report | Threshold / trigger | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Currency Transaction Report (CTR) | Cash in or out over USD 10,000 by or for one person in one business day | 15 calendar days |
| Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) | Suspicious activity of USD 5,000+ with a suspect; USD 25,000+ regardless of suspect; any amount for insider abuse | 30 days from detection (60 days if no suspect identified) |
| Continuing-activity SAR | Ongoing suspicious activity | Every 90 days, then filed within 120 days of the previous SAR |
| OFAC blocking report | Property blocked | 10 business days |
| OFAC reject report | Transaction rejected | 10 business days |
| Annual report of blocked property | Held as of 30 June | 30 September |
| FinCEN 314(a) requests | Search results | 14 days |

**SAR confidentiality:** associates must never disclose that a SAR has been or may be filed ("tipping off"). This is a federal crime.

## 6. Monetary instrument and recordkeeping rules
- Cash purchases of cashier's checks or money orders of **USD 3,000 - 10,000** require ID and are logged (31 CFR 1010.415).
- Funds transfers of **USD 3,000+** keep originator and beneficiary information (Travel Rule, 31 CFR 1010.410).
- Records are retained for **5 years**.

## 7. OFAC sanctions program
- Real-time screening of wires, ACH (IAT), Zelle, account openings and the customer base (daily rescreen against list updates).
- Potential matches are reviewed within 1 business day. True matches are blocked or rejected and reported to OFAC.
- Atlas does not process transactions involving comprehensively sanctioned jurisdictions (e.g. Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and the Crimea, so-called DNR and LNR regions of Ukraine) except as licensed.

Unusual activity is referred through the **UAR (Unusual Activity Referral) form** on AtlasNet within 1 business day of detection. FIU hotline (internal): ext. 5-0150.
