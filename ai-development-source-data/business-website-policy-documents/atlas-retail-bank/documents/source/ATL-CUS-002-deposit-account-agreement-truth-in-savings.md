## 1. Agreement
This agreement, the Schedule of Fees (ATL-CUS-003), the Funds Availability Policy (ATL-CUS-004), the Electronic Fund Transfer Disclosure (ATL-CUS-005) and the Privacy Notice (ATL-CUS-006) together form the contract between you and Atlas Retail Bank, N.A. ("Atlas", "we"). By signing a signature card, opening an account online or using the account, you agree to these terms. This agreement is governed by federal law and the laws of the **State of Maryland**, except where the law of your state of residence requires otherwise.

### 2.1 How interest is calculated
- Interest is calculated on the **daily balance** method, using a daily periodic rate applied to the principal each day.
- Interest begins to accrue on the business day we receive credit for non-cash deposits (e.g. checks).
- Interest compounds daily and is credited **monthly** (savings and money market) or monthly / at maturity (CDs, as you choose).

### 2.2 Certificates of deposit
- **Early withdrawal penalty:** terms of 12 months or less, 90 days' interest; terms over 12 months, 180 days' interest. The penalty may reduce principal.
- **Automatic renewal:** CDs renew for the same term at the rate then in effect. You have a **10-day grace period** after maturity to withdraw without penalty. We will send a maturity notice at least 30 days before maturity.

## 3. Ownership and beneficiaries
- **Individual** and **joint** accounts (joint with right of survivorship unless state law or your instructions say otherwise).
- **Payable-on-death (POD)** beneficiaries may be named.
- **Trust** and **custodial (UTMA)** accounts require supporting documents.
- **FDIC insurance (illustrative):** deposits are insured up to USD 250,000 per depositor, per ownership category. *(Atlas is fictional and its deposits are not actually insured.)*

## 4. Deposits and withdrawals
- We may refuse, limit or return any deposit. Checks payable to others need proper endorsement: "For Mobile Deposit Only at Atlas" for mobile deposits.
- Withdrawals may require ID. For cash withdrawals over USD 10,000, 1 business day's notice is requested.
- **Savings and money market:** Atlas no longer limits the number of withdrawals per month. A USD 10 excessive-withdrawal fee applies after 6 withdrawals in a statement cycle.

## 5. Overdrafts and NSF
- Atlas **does not charge NSF fees** for returned items.
- **Overdraft fee:** USD 25 per item, up to 2 per day. There is no fee if the account is overdrawn by USD 50 or less at the end of the day, or for items of USD 5 or less.
- One-time debit card and ATM transactions are approved into overdraft **only if you opt in** (Regulation E, 12 CFR 1005.17).
- **Overdraft Protection Transfer** from a linked savings account: no fee.
- **Grace period:** if you bring your balance back to at least zero by 11:59 pm ET the next business day, the overdraft fee is reversed.

## 6. Order of posting
Each business day we post:
1. deposits;
2. debit card, ATM and Zelle transactions in time order;
3. checks in check-number order;
4. ACH debits and fees.

## 7. Statements and your duty to review
You will receive monthly statements (quarterly for savings with no electronic transfers). Report errors in checks or unauthorised signatures within **60 days** of the statement date. For electronic transfers, see ATL-CUS-005. Atlas is not liable for forged items that you fail to report in time (UCC 4-406).

## 8. Stop payments, legal process, setoff
- Stop payments are valid for 6 months and may be renewed (fee in ATL-CUS-003).
- We will comply with garnishments, levies and subpoenas. Federal benefits protected under 31 CFR Part 212 (2 months of direct-deposited benefits) are not frozen.
- Atlas may **set off** funds in your deposit accounts against debts you owe us, except where prohibited (e.g. credit card debts under Regulation Z).

## 9. Dormant accounts and unclaimed property
An account with no customer-initiated activity for 12 months becomes **inactive**; after 24 months it becomes **dormant**. Balances are sent to the state unclaimed property office after the period set by your state's law. This is usually 3 years (e.g. NY, NJ, MD, MA, FL, NC). We will notify you by mail and email before doing this.

## 10. Closing accounts
You may close an account at any time. We may close your account with 30 days' notice, or immediately in cases of fraud, abuse or legal requirement. Accounts closed within 90 days of opening may be charged a USD 25 early-closure fee.

## 11. Arbitration and waiver of class actions
Disputes may be resolved by binding arbitration administered by the AAA under its Consumer Arbitration Rules. You may **opt out** within 60 days of account opening by writing to Atlas Legal, 1450 Harborview Avenue, Baltimore, MD 21202. Small-claims court remains available.

## 12. Changes to this agreement
We will give you at least **30 days' notice** of adverse changes (21 days for fees under Regulation E, where applicable).
