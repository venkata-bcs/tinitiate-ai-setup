## 1. Commitment
Atlas makes credit available to all qualified applicants on fair and equal terms. We do not discriminate on a **prohibited basis** in any aspect of a credit transaction, including marketing, application, underwriting, pricing, servicing and collections.

## 2. Applicable laws
- **Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) / Regulation B**
- **Fair Housing Act**
- **Home Mortgage Disclosure Act (HMDA) / Regulation C**
- **Community Reinvestment Act (CRA)**, under the 2023 interagency final rule as applicable
- **Truth in Lending Act (TILA) / Regulation Z**, including the Ability-to-Repay / Qualified Mortgage rules
- **RESPA / Regulation X** and the **TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosures (TRID)**
- **Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA)** and the **Military Lending Act (MLA)**
- **UDAAP** prohibitions (Dodd-Frank Sections 1031 and 1036)
- State fair-lending and anti-predatory lending laws (e.g. NY Exec. Law 296-a, NJ Home Ownership Security Act, NC Anti-Predatory Lending Law, MA Community Investment rules)

## 3. Prohibited bases
| Federal (ECOA / FHA) | Additional state protections in the Atlas footprint |
|---|---|
| Race, colour, religion, national origin, sex (incl. sexual orientation and gender identity), marital status, age (if the applicant can contract), receipt of public assistance income, good-faith exercise of consumer credit rights, familial status and handicap (housing) | Military status, source of income (e.g. NY, NJ, MA, MD, CT), genetic information, ancestry, creed, height and weight (in some cities) |

## 4. Underwriting standards
- Decisions are based on **creditworthiness factors** only: credit history, capacity (DTI), capital, collateral and conditions.
- Standard maximum ratios: personal loans 45% DTI; auto loans 120% LTV; mortgages per Qualified Mortgage guidelines (generally 43% DTI, with compensating factors up to 50%).
- Alternative credit data (rent, utilities, cash-flow underwriting) may be considered **at the applicant's request** to help applicants with thin credit files.
- **Exceptions** to policy need second-level approval and are monitored quarterly for disparate impact.

## 5. Pricing controls
- Rate sheets are set centrally. Loan officers may give pricing concessions only within a ±0.125% band, and must document the reason (competitive match, relationship).
- Loan officer compensation does not vary with loan terms (Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.36).
- Quarterly regression analysis of pricing and approvals by prohibited basis (using BISG proxy where data is not collected).

## 6. Marketing and redlining prevention
- Marketing campaigns are reviewed by Fair Lending before launch to make sure they reach majority-minority census tracts in proportion.
- Branch openings and closings are assessed for impact on low- and moderate-income (LMI) and majority-minority communities.
- **Special Purpose Credit Program:** the "Atlas Home Start" down-payment grant of up to USD 15,000 is available in designated census tracts in Baltimore, Philadelphia, Newark, Hartford and Richmond.

## 7. Adverse action notices
When credit is denied, offered on less favourable terms, or an account is terminated, Atlas sends a written adverse action notice **within 30 days** of a complete application. The notice includes:
- the **specific principal reasons** for the action (up to 4)
- the ECOA anti-discrimination notice, naming the OCC as the federal regulator
- the credit score used, its range and key factors (FCRA Section 615)
- the name of the consumer reporting agency and the applicant's right to a free report

*ECOA notice:* "The Federal Equal Credit Opportunity Act prohibits creditors from discriminating against credit applicants on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age (provided the applicant has the capacity to enter into a binding contract); because all or part of the applicant's income derives from any public assistance program; or because the applicant has in good faith exercised any right under the Consumer Credit Protection Act."

## 8. Servicing, collections and hardship
- Hardship programs include payment deferral (up to 3 months), loan modification and forbearance. They are offered consistently using objective eligibility criteria.
- Collections follow FDCPA standards (even though Atlas is a creditor collecting its own debts): no calls before 8:00 am or after 9:00 pm, and no more than 7 calls in 7 days per debt (Regulation F as a benchmark).
- **SCRA:** interest is capped at 6% on pre-service debts for active-duty servicemembers. Foreclosure protections apply.
