## 1. Important information about opening a new account
To help the government fight the funding of terrorism and money laundering, federal law requires all financial institutions to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each person who opens an account.

**What this means for you:** when you open an account, we will ask for your name, address, date of birth and other information that will allow us to identify you. We may also ask to see your driver's licence or other identifying documents.

*(USA PATRIOT Act Section 326; 31 CFR 1020.220)*

## 2. Personal accounts - information required

| Information | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Full legal name | As on government ID |
| Date of birth | Must be 18+ to own an account alone; 13-17 with an adult co-owner (Atlas Student Checking) |
| Residential address | A physical street address (no P.O. Box); APO/FPO accepted for military |
| Taxpayer ID | SSN or ITIN for US persons; passport number and country for non-US persons |
| Contact details | Mobile phone and email (for alerts and e-statements) |
| Employment / source of funds | Occupation and expected monthly activity |

### 2.1 Acceptable identification
**Primary ID (one required, unexpired, with photo):**
- US driver's licence or state ID card
- US passport or passport card
- Foreign passport (with or without visa)
- US military ID
- Permanent Resident Card (Green Card)
- Matricula Consular (accepted at selected branches; secondary ID required)
- IDNYC (New York City branches only; secondary ID required)

**Secondary ID (when required):**
- Major credit card
- Utility bill dated within 60 days
- Student ID
- Employer ID
- Medicare card
- Voter registration card

### 2.2 Minors and custodial accounts
UTMA/UGMA custodial accounts need the custodian's ID and the minor's SSN and birth certificate. Atlas Student Checking (ages 13-17) needs a parent or guardian as joint owner.

## 3. Business accounts
| Entity type | Documents |
|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship | Owner ID; EIN or SSN; DBA / assumed-name certificate (if applicable) |
| General partnership | Partnership agreement; EIN; ID for all partners with signing authority |
| LLC | Articles of Organization; Operating Agreement; EIN letter (IRS CP 575 / 147C); certificate of good standing |
| Corporation | Articles of Incorporation; corporate resolution; EIN; certificate of good standing |
| Non-profit | Articles; bylaws; IRS 501(c) determination letter; board resolution |

### 3.1 Beneficial ownership
Under the FinCEN Customer Due Diligence Rule (31 CFR 1010.230), legal-entity customers must certify:
- **each individual who owns 25% or more** of the equity, and
- **one individual with significant control** (e.g. CEO, CFO, managing member).

We verify the identity of each person listed. Atlas also collects this information independently of the Corporate Transparency Act, which no longer applies to US-formed companies after the March 2025 FinCEN interim final rule.

## 4. How we verify
- Documentary verification: the ID is inspected in branch, or scanned with a liveness selfie online.
- Non-documentary verification: identity is checked against consumer reporting agencies, public records and ChexSystems / Early Warning Services.
- OFAC and sanctions screening against the SDN and other US government lists.
- Customer risk rating (low / moderate / high). High-risk customers receive Enhanced Due Diligence: money services businesses, cash-intensive businesses, third-party payment processors, foreign PEPs, and marijuana-related businesses (not accepted).

## 5. When we may decline or close an account
Atlas may decline to open an account if:
- identity cannot be verified within **30 days** (online opening: 10 days, then the account is closed and funds returned by check)
- the applicant matches a sanctions list
- ChexSystems shows unpaid losses from account abuse or fraud in the past 5 years (losses under USD 500 that are resolved do not disqualify; we offer Atlas Fresh Start Checking)
- the information supplied is false or inconsistent

If we decline your application based on a consumer report, we will send you an adverse action notice. It names the reporting agency and explains your right to a free copy of the report within 60 days, and your right to dispute it (Fair Credit Reporting Act).

Account-opening documents are available in English and Spanish. Interpreter services in 200+ languages are available free of charge in branch and by phone. Customers with disabilities can request large-print, braille or accessible digital documents.
