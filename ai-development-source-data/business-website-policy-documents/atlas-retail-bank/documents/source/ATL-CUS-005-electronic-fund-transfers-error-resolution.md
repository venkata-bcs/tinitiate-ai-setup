## 1. Types of transfers covered
- Debit card purchases (in store, online, contactless and mobile wallets)
- ATM withdrawals, deposits, transfers and balance enquiries
- Preauthorised credits (direct deposit) and preauthorised debits (ACH bill payments)
- Online and mobile banking transfers and Bill Pay
- Zelle person-to-person payments
- Electronic check conversion (a merchant converting your paper check into an EFT)

## 2. Limits

|---|---|---|

## 3. Your liability for unauthorised transfers
**Tell us AT ONCE** if you believe your card, PIN or online credentials have been lost or stolen, or that someone has transferred money without your permission. Telephoning is the best way to limit your losses.

| When you notify us | Your maximum liability |
|---|---|
| Within 2 business days of learning of the loss or theft | USD 50 |
| After 2 business days but within 60 days of the statement | USD 500 |
| More than 60 days after the statement showing the transfer | Unlimited for transfers after the 60 days that we could have prevented |

**Atlas Zero Liability:** for debit card transactions processed on the Visa network, you are not liable for unauthorised transactions if you report them promptly and were not grossly negligent.

## 4. Error resolution
Call or write as soon as you can if you think your statement or receipt is wrong, or if you need more information about a transfer. We must hear from you **no later than 60 days** after we sent the **first** statement on which the problem appeared. Please tell us:
1. Your name and account number.
2. The error or the transfer you are unsure about, and why you believe it is an error or need more information.
3. The dollar amount of the suspected error.

If you tell us orally, we may ask you to send your complaint or question in writing within 10 business days.

### 4.1 Investigation timelines

| Step | Timeframe |
|---|---|
| Determine whether an error occurred | 10 business days (20 for new accounts open < 30 days) |
| If more time is needed: provisional credit | Within 10 business days (20 for new accounts) |
| Extended investigation | Up to 45 days (90 days for POS, foreign-initiated, or new-account transfers) |
| Written results | Within 3 business days after completing the investigation |

If we decide there was no error, we will send you a written explanation and reverse any provisional credit, with 5 business days' notice. You may ask for copies of the documents we used in our investigation.

## 5. Zelle and scams
- Zelle payments to someone you authorise are generally **not** reversible, even if you were tricked into sending them.
- Atlas voluntarily reimburses eligible customers for **imposter scams**: payments sent to criminals pretending to be Atlas, a government agency or a utility, if reported within 30 days.
- Only use Zelle with people you know and trust. **Atlas will never call you and ask you to send money to yourself or to "protect" your account.**

## 6. Preauthorised payments
- **Stopping a payment:** tell us at least 3 business days before the scheduled date. A USD 15 stop-payment fee applies (ATL-CUS-003).
- **Notice of varying amounts:** the payee must notify you 10 days before a payment that varies in amount.
- **Our liability:** if we fail to stop a payment when properly instructed, we will be liable for your losses or damages.

## 7. Our liability for failure to make transfers
If we do not complete a transfer on time or in the correct amount, we will be liable for your losses or damages, with some exceptions. For example, we are not liable if:
- you do not have enough funds
- the ATM did not have enough cash
- circumstances beyond our control prevented the transfer

## 8. Confidentiality
We will disclose information to third parties about your account or transfers only:
- where necessary to complete transfers
- to verify the existence and condition of your account for a third party, such as a credit bureau or merchant
- to comply with government agency or court orders
- if you give us written permission
- as described in our Privacy Notice (ATL-CUS-006)
