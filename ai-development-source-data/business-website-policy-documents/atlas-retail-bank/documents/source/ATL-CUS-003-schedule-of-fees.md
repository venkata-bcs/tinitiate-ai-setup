## 1. Monthly maintenance fees
See ATL-CUS-002 for monthly fees and how to avoid them. Fees are charged on the last business day of the statement cycle.

## 2. Overdraft and returned items

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Overdraft item paid | USD 25 (max 2 per day; none if overdrawn by USD 50 or less) |
| Returned item (NSF) | **USD 0** |
| Overdraft Protection Transfer | USD 0 |
| Extended overdraft fee | USD 0 (discontinued 2023) |

## 3. ATM and debit card

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Atlas ATMs (540 across the East Coast) | Free |
| Non-Atlas ATM in the US | USD 3.00 per withdrawal, plus any owner surcharge |
| Non-Atlas ATM outside the US | USD 5.00, plus 3% of the amount |
| Foreign transaction fee (debit card purchases) | 3% of the USD amount |
| Replacement debit card (standard) | Free |
| Rush card delivery | USD 25 |
| ATM fee refunds - Premier Checking | Up to USD 20 per statement cycle |

## 4. Wire transfers

| Service | Domestic | International |
|---|---|---|
| Incoming wire | USD 15 | USD 16 |
| Outgoing wire - online | USD 25 | USD 40 (USD 5 if sent in foreign currency) |
| Outgoing wire - branch | USD 30 | USD 45 |
| Wire recall / amendment | USD 25 | USD 35 |

International wires sent in foreign currency include an exchange-rate margin. The rate and fees are disclosed before you send, as required by the Remittance Transfer Rule (Regulation E Subpart B). You may cancel within 30 minutes of payment.

## 7. Fee waivers
- **Military:** active-duty service members and veterans with Atlas Everyday Checking pay no monthly fee and receive 2 non-Atlas ATM fee waivers per cycle.
- **Seniors (65+):** no monthly fee on Everyday Checking and free paper statements.
- **Students (13-24):** no monthly fee on Student or Everyday Checking.

## 8. State-specific notes
- **New York:** basic banking accounts are available under NY Banking Law Section 14-e with a monthly fee of no more than USD 3.
- **New Jersey:** lifeline checking accounts are available under N.J.S.A. 17:16N.
- **Massachusetts:** customers aged 65+ and under 18 receive free basic savings and checking (M.G.L. c.167D).
- **Maryland and Pennsylvania:** state-specific fee notices are available in branch.
