About Atlas

A community-minded national bank headquartered in Baltimore, serving the East Coast for nearly a century.

Our story

Atlas began in 1927 as Atlas Mutual Savings, a savings bank founded by Baltimore dock workers and shopkeepers. It survived the Depression by never closing its doors, and became known as the bank "that holds up the neighbourhood".

Through careful mergers with community banks in Pennsylvania, New York, New England and the Carolinas, Atlas has grown to USD 48.6 billion in assets. Each branch still has local lending authority and a community advisory board.

Mission: Help families and small businesses on the East Coast build financial strength - honestly, simply and close to home.
