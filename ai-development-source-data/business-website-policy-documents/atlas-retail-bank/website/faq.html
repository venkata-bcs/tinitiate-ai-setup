Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about Atlas accounts and services.

What ID do I need to open an account?

One unexpired government photo ID such as a driver's licence, passport or Green Card. Some IDs (e.g. Matricula Consular, IDNYC) need a secondary ID. See ATL-CUS-001.

When will my deposited check be available?

Usually on the next business day. The first USD 275 is always available next day. Larger or unusual deposits may be held up to 7 business days. See ATL-CUS-004.

Does Atlas charge overdraft fees?

USD 25 per item, up to 2 per day. There is no fee if you are overdrawn by USD 50 or less, and no NSF fees at all. Debit card overdrafts need your opt-in.

Someone used my debit card. What do I do?

Lock your card in Atlas Mobile and call 1-800-555-0142 right away. Report within 2 business days to limit liability to USD 50; Zero Liability usually means USD 0. See ATL-CUS-005.

Can I get my money back if I was scammed on Zelle?

Payments you authorised are generally not reversible, but Atlas reimburses eligible imposter scams reported within 30 days. Atlas will never ask you to send money to yourself.

How do I stop Atlas sharing my information with affiliates?

Call 1-800-555-0148, visit the privacy page, or mail the opt-out form in our Privacy Notice (ATL-CUS-006).

What happens to an account I don't use?

After 12 months without activity it becomes inactive; after 24 months it is dormant. After about 3 years (varies by state), funds go to the state unclaimed property office.

Do branches close for hurricanes or snowstorms?

Branches may close for public-safety reasons. Closures are posted in Atlas Mobile and on the Alerts page; ATMs and digital banking remain available.
